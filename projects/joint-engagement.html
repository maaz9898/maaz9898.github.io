Automated Assessment of Joint Engagement in Children

Video models that measure social engagement between two children from a single camera.

  • Video understanding
  • Pose
  • Gaze
  • PyTorch
Role
Lead researcher
Where
A-Sense, HBKU, Doha
Paper
STIN, EUVIP 2026
Stack
PyTorch, pose and gaze models

Problem

Joint engagement between children is a key behavioural measure in autism research. It is currently rated manually from video, one window at a time, which is slow and difficult to scale.

Generated examples. No frame shows a real participant.

Input Signals

Generated frame. A journal paper on the 3D pipeline is in preparation.

Model: STIN

STIN represents each child with a stream of pose, gaze and appearance features. Cross-attention between the two streams is guided by the distance and orientation between the pair.
